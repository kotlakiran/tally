Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Tally app ("Tally", "we", "us") handles your information. Tally is designed to be private and on‑device‑first. By using Tally you agree to this policy.

1. Data stored on your device

Your habits, counts, meals, reminders and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit this data to our own servers. We do not have our own backend that stores your personal data.

2. Optional Google Drive backup

If you turn on "Back up to Google Drive", Tally saves a copy of your app data to a private, hidden folder (appDataFolder) in your own Google Drive. This backup is tied to your Google account and is not accessible to us or to other apps. You can turn it off or delete the backup at any time. This feature is off by default and requires your explicit sign‑in and permission (scope drive.appdata).

3. Health Connect data (optional)

If you connect Health Connect, Tally accesses and reads the following health data types on your device: steps, active energy burned, sleep sessions and duration, and hydration volume. Tally does not write or modify Health Connect data. This access is optional, read-only, and requires your permission in Android Health Connect.

Tally collects a temporary summary of these metrics only to show daily health context and personalize in-app habit and coaching suggestions. Tally does not collect health data for advertising, profiling, sale, or unrelated purposes. Health Connect data is not stored on Tally's servers and is not retained as a separate health-data history by Tally.

Health Connect data leaves your device only when you actively use the AI coach by tapping to ask a question. At that moment, a brief summary of the permitted metrics (for example, "steps today: 5,000; sleep: 7h") is sent to Google's Gemini model via Firebase AI Logic solely to generate that one coaching response. If you do not use the AI coach, no Health Connect data is transmitted anywhere. Your health data is never sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties for their own purposes. You can decline or revoke Health Connect permission at any time; revocation stops future access immediately.

4. AI coach (Google Gemini via Firebase AI Logic)

When you ask the in‑app coach a question, your message and a small amount of context (such as a summary of today's logged meals and health metrics) are sent to Google's Gemini model via Firebase AI Logic to generate a response. This processing is subject to Google's / Firebase's privacy terms. We do not use your inputs to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell your data.

5. Notifications

Tally may send local reminder notifications (for habits and meals) that you schedule. These are generated on your device.

6. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use third‑party advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

7. Data deletion and retention

You can delete your data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. If you enabled Google Drive backup, you can delete the backup from your Google Drive or by turning the feature off. Revoking Health Connect permission stops future access, and Tally does not retain a separate copy of Health Connect data.

8. Children

Tally is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly.
